⚙️ How It Works

Built by the community,
for the community

A simple, transparent process to keep La Union accommodations safe and scammer-free.

1
Establishments Register
Legitimate resorts, inns, and hotels submit their business permits, official payment channels, and contact details for review.
2
Ground Verification
Our volunteer team and LGU partners physically verify each business before issuing the SafeStay Verified badge.
3
Travelers Check First
Before paying, tourists look up the establishment or payment number here to confirm it's legitimate.
4
Community Reports Scams
Victims file reports. All reports are reviewed and added to the scam database within 24 hours.
5
Alerts Go Out
Flagged scam details broadcast through Facebook, SMS alerts, and downloadable warning posters.
6
LGU & PNP Coordination
Confirmed scam cases forwarded to local government and PNP La Union for legal action.
